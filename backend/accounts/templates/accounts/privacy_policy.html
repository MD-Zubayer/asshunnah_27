✓ You can customize or disable each notification channel independently in Settings.
🎧 Media Playback Foreground Service FOREGROUND_SERVICE FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK
When used: Allows seamless background listening to Holy Quran recitation (audio tilawat of all 114 Surahs) and Islamic lectures while your screen is off or while using another app.
✓ A persistent player notification with pause/play controls is displayed as required by Android guidelines.

5 User-Generated Content (UGC) & Community Safety

Because our application provides interactive messaging and calling features for consultation, we maintain a Zero-Tolerance Policy for objectionable, abusive, or harmful behavior:

Mandatory Safety Features Built Into the App:
  • Block User: Any user can instantly block any contact from within the chat header menu. Blocking immediately halts all incoming messages, calls, and interactions from that person.
  • Report User & Content: Users can flag objectionable content or abusive users with one tap. Reports are automatically forwarded to our administrative team.
  • Swift Moderation Action: Reported content is investigated within 24 hours. Accounts found violating community guidelines (harassment, hate speech, spam, vulgarity) are permanently banned.

7 Data Security & Encryption Standards

We implement rigorous technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of network traffic between your mobile app and our servers is strictly encrypted using modern TLS / HTTPS and secure WebSockets (WSS).
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are hashed using one-way cryptographic algorithms (PBKDF2/SHA-256) with unique per-user salts.
  • Isolated Private Diaries: Your Islamic diary reflections, mood tracking, and Zakat calculations are accessible solely by your authenticated credentials.
